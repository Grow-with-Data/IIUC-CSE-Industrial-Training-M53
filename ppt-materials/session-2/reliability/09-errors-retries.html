Errors & retries

The SDKs already retry transient errors (429 / 5xx) with backoff. Your job is to catch what's left and decide.

the safety net you write
oai = OpenAI(max_retries=3, timeout=30)

try:
    resp = oai.responses.create(…)
except openai.RateLimitError:
    # slow down / retry later
except openai.APIStatusError as e:
    # e.status_code (5xx …)
except openai.APIConnectionError:
    # your network dropped
429

Rate limit

“All our agents are busy, please hold.” Wait and redial — the SDK does this for you.

→ auto-retried with backoff
5xx

Server error

The provider's switchboard glitched. Transient — also auto-retried.

→ auto-retried with backoff

Your own Wi-Fi dropped. Catch it and decide what to do.
