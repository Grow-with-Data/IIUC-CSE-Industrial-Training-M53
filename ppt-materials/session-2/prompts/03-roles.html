Messages & roles

A chat model reads a conversation of roles. The system prompt is where you set the character before anyone speaks.

system
Who it is & the rules. Persona, tone, guardrails — applies to every turn.
user
The person. The actual question or data for this turn.
assistant
The model's replies. Its earlier lines in the conversation.
the conversation
🎬 You are a calm support agent for Northstar. Answer in one sentence.
A customer asks how to reset their password.
Sure — head to Settings → Security and click “Reset password,” then follow the emailed link.
🎭
Real-life: a theatre script. system is the director's note defining the character; user lines are the customer; assistant lines are what our character already said.
