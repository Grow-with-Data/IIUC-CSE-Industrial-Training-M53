Anatomy of a system prompt

Most production prompts are assembled from the same parts. You rarely need all eight — but decide on each one consciously.

01

Role / persona

Who it is.

“You are a support agent for Northstar.”
02

Task / goal

What to do.

“Triage the message & draft a reply.”
03

Tone / audience

How it sounds.

“Friendly, calm, and concise.”
04

Rules / guardrails

The do's & don'ts.

“Never invent account details.”
05

Output format

Shape of the answer.

“Reply in 2–3 sentences.”
06

Context / knowledge

Facts to use.

Hours, product names, policies.
07

Fallback

What to do when unsure.

“If unsure, offer to escalate.”
08

Examples (opt.)

One or two model answers.

“Few-shot” for tricky formats.
◆
Edges matter. Put the most important rules first and last — models weight the edges most.
◆
Be specific & positive. “Reply in 2–3 sentences” beats “be brief.”
◆
Keep it stable. Anything that changes per request belongs in the user message.
