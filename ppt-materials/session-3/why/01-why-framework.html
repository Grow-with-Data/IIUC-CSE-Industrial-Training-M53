The motivation

Why a framework? Because we keep writing the same thing three times.

Session 2 — one job (“send a message, read the reply”), written for three SDKs:
OpenAI
oai.responses.create(input=…)
resp.output_text
Gemini
gem.models.generate_content(contents=…)
resp.text
Anthropic
ant.messages.create(messages=…)
resp.content[0].text
Different method names · different arguments · three different ways to read one reply. Now multiply that by structured output, tokens, errors…

A framework — you gain

  • + One interface across every provider
  • + Reusable blocks: models, messages, tools, memory
  • + A straight path to agents, RAG & tracing later

…you pay

  • – A dependency to install & keep updated
  • – A layer of abstraction to learn
  • – Occasionally “it did something I didn’t expect”
rule of thumb Use a framework when the shared interface saves you more than the abstraction costs. A one-off script? Raw SDK is fine. An app that grows every week — like ours? It earns its keep.
